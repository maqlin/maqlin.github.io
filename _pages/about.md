I'm a final-year PhD student at the [Department of Astronomy, Tsinghua University](https://astro.tsinghua.edu.cn). I'm a theorist aiming to extract key physical quantities from large datasets to study galaxy formation and the dark matter haloes that host them.

[Click here](/research/) to learn more about my research.

Before coming to Tsinghua, I received my B.S. from the [School of Physics and Astronomy, Sun Yat-sen University](https://spa.sysu.edu.cn/zh-hans). From November 2025 to July 2026, I visited [Leiden Observatory](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/science/leiden-observatory) in the Netherlands. In my spare time, I play table tennis and badminton—I have competed in the Beijing Intercollegiate Table Tennis Team Championships—and I enjoy hiking and city walking.
